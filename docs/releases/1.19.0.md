# 1.19.0: compact pill, clickable alerts and Hermes costs

This is a feature release. It does not change provider billing, credential
formats or stored history. Version authority remains `plugin.json`. The exact
published release body comes from the matching section in `CHANGELOG.md`.

## User-visible changes

- **Smaller DankBar pill** ([#33](https://github.com/bernardopg/AiOverviewControl/issues/33)):
  two new Interface settings. **Provider names in pill** (`pillShowNames`,
  on by default) can be turned off to keep only each provider's logo and
  percentage. **Compact pill spacing** (`pillCompact`, off by default)
  tightens the gap around the separator dot and inside each entry. Both
  change only the bar pill; the hover tooltip still names every provider.
  See [configuration](../configuration.md).
- **Clickable quota alerts:** clicking a quota notification, or its
  **Open dashboard** button, opens the popout on the provider that crossed
  the threshold, expands its card and scrolls to it. An already-open popout
  stays open. The same action is available to scripts and compositor
  bindings as `dms ipc call aiOverviewControl focus <provider-id>`, which
  returns `PROVIDER_FOCUSED` or `UNKNOWN_PROVIDER`. See
  [usage](../usage.md#ipc-commands).
- **Hermes costs:** Hermes prices requests upstream, so its local ledger
  often records usage without a cost and the card showed `—`. Those rows are
  now priced per model at LiteLLM list price. Subscription (`included`)
  usage stays free and costs already in the ledger are kept. When every day
  of the week is priced and some of it is paid, the 7-day chart plots cost
  instead of tokens.
- **Settings window:** hovering a toggle row no longer clips the left edge of
  its highlight.

## Privacy and network

- The Hermes pricing reuses the LiteLLM table the Claude adapter already
  downloads from `raw.githubusercontent.com`, at most once a day, as a plain
  unauthenticated GET. A trimmed copy is cached as
  `~/.cache/AiOverviewControl/litellm-prices.json`. `AIOC_NO_LITELLM=1`
  disables the download for both adapters; Hermes then reuses the last
  snapshot, or leaves unresolved costs unknown.
- The notification click runs `dms ipc call` locally. Only plain provider ids
  (`[A-Za-z0-9_.-]+`) are passed to it.

## How the notification click works

`providers/send-quota-alert` still deduplicates alerts across bars, reloads
and restarts under a file lock. For each alert it now keeps one
`notify-send --action` process waiting for the click, because the
notification daemon reports an action only to the process that owns the
notification. That process:

- does not hold the deduplication lock, so other alerts are never delayed;
- is killed when the same alert is re-sent (a reminder or a critical
  escalation), so reminders do not accumulate processes;
- exits when the notification is closed, expires or is replaced.

A click on an entry in the DMS notification history after that process has
exited is not routed back to the plugin.

## Maintenance and validation

- The full native DMS UI suite now runs in CI under headless sway against a
  pinned DankMaterialShell checkout, and fails instead of skipping when a
  prerequisite is missing. It covers the widget, both pills, the dashboard,
  settings persistence/reset, the settings window, currency bindings, the
  pointer drag and the new `focus` IPC call.
- `tests/test-quota-alert.sh` covers click routing, lock release, stale-waiter
  cleanup and unsafe ids. `tests/test-local-analytics.sh` covers unknown,
  estimated, included, `:free` and unpriced Hermes models, with and without a
  price table. All 24 suites run offline.
- Measured the Claude transcript parse before optimizing it: 0.26 s per
  refresh, 0.43 s after a 20 MB active transcript changed. The incremental
  parse stays unimplemented until that becomes slow.

## Limitations

- LiteLLM list prices do not reflect negotiated, discounted or regional rates.
- Hermes models without a LiteLLM entry, such as local Ollama tags, keep
  their totals unknown.
- With several bars, the popout opens on the bar instance that owns the IPC
  target, as with `toggle`.
